# How to File a Consumer Complaint Online in India

If you bought a faulty product or received poor service, the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** lets you file a complaint without a long, expensive court process. Most of it can now be done online.

## Step 1: Try the National Consumer Helpline first

Before filing a formal complaint, contact the **National Consumer Helpline (1915)**. Many disputes are settled at this stage, with the company responding once the helpline takes up the grievance.

## Step 2: Choose the right Consumer Commission

Where you file depends on the value of the goods or services you paid for:

| Value of claim | Where to file |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹50 lakh | District Consumer Commission |
| ₹50 lakh to ₹2 crore | State Consumer Commission |

## Step 3: Check the fee

There is **no filing fee at the District Commission for claims up to ₹5 lakh**. This makes small consumer disputes practically free to file.

## Step 4: File within 2 years, online on e-Jagriti

A consumer complaint should generally be filed **within 2 years** of the problem arising. You can file online on the government's **e-Jagriti** portal, upload documents and track the case.

## Documents to keep ready

- Bill, invoice or receipt
- Warranty card or terms of service
- Emails, chats or letters with the seller or service provider
- Photos or videos of the defect, if any

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an advocate to file a consumer complaint?**
No, you can file it yourself. Some people consult an advocate to draft the complaint or when the matter is complex.

**Can I file a complaint online?**
Yes, on the e-Jagriti portal.
