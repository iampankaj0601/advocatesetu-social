# Senior Citizens Maintenance Act: Your Rights as a Parent

Looking after elderly parents is more than a moral duty in India. The **Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007** makes it a legal obligation and gives parents and senior citizens a quick remedy through a special **Maintenance Tribunal**.

## Who can claim maintenance

- **Parents** — biological, adoptive or step-parents, and grandparents — can claim from their **adult children**. A parent does not need to be a senior citizen to claim.
- A **childless senior citizen** (aged **60 or above**) can claim from an adult **relative** who is in possession of, or would inherit, their property.
- The claim is available to a person who cannot maintain themselves from their own earnings or property.

Maintenance covers needs such as food, clothing, residence, medical care and treatment, so that the parent can lead a normal life.

## How to apply

The application is filed before the **Maintenance Tribunal**. It can be filed by the senior citizen or parent, or, if they are unable to, by a person or organisation they authorise. The Tribunal can also take up a case on its own.

| Point | What the Act says |
|---|---|
| Interim support | The Tribunal can order a monthly interim allowance while the case is pending |
| Time to decide | Within **90 days** of service of notice; extendable **once by up to 30 days** in exceptional cases |
| Maximum amount | Up to **₹10,000 a month**, as prescribed by State rules |

## Property given on the promise of care

If a senior citizen transferred property (by gift or otherwise) on the condition that the person receiving it would provide basic amenities and care, and that person refuses or fails to do so, the **Tribunal can declare the transfer void** at the senior citizen's option.

## Abandonment is an offence

Anyone responsible for a senior citizen who leaves them somewhere intending to abandon them can face **imprisonment of up to 3 months, a fine of up to ₹5,000, or both**.

## Help for senior citizens: Elderline 14567

**Elderline 14567** is the toll-free National Helpline for Senior Citizens, run by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment. It provides information, guidance and emotional support, and coordinates with local agencies such as police, legal aid and welfare departments.

## Keep these records

- Proof of age (for senior citizens)
- Proof of relationship with the children or relative
- Details of your income, expenses and medical costs
- Copies of any gift deed or transfer document, and any written promise of care

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a parent below 60 claim maintenance?**
Yes. Parents can claim from adult children whether or not they are senior citizens, if they cannot maintain themselves.

**Is maintenance always ₹10,000 a month?**
No. ₹10,000 is the upper limit in the Act. The amount depends on the facts and on the State's rules.

**Can the Tribunal give support before the final order?**
Yes. It can order interim monthly maintenance while the case is pending.
