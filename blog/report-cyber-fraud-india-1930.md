# How to Report Cyber Fraud in India

Online fraud moves fast, so your response should too. If you or someone you know loses money to a cyber scam, act within the first hours.

## 1. Call 1930 immediately

The **National Cyber Crime Helpline is 1930**. Reporting early improves the chance of the money being stopped before it is moved out of the fraudster's account.

## 2. File a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in

Register a complaint on the **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in)**. Keep your complaint number safe.

## 3. Inform your bank

Call your bank's official helpline and ask them to block the card, account or UPI access that was misused.

## 4. Beware of "digital arrest" scams

Police, CBI or ED **do not arrest people over video calls**. A so-called "digital arrest", where callers in uniform demand money to "clear your name", is a scam. Disconnect and report it.

## 5. Report suspicious calls and messages

Fraud calls, SMS and WhatsApp messages can be reported on the **Chakshu** facility of the government's **Sanchar Saathi** portal.

## Keep these records

- Screenshots of messages, payment confirmations and the fraudster's profile
- Transaction IDs and the time of each transaction
- Phone numbers, UPI IDs or websites used

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a deadline to report cyber fraud?**
Report as soon as possible. The faster you report, the better the chance of stopping the money.

**Can I report on behalf of a family member?**
Yes, you can help them call 1930 and file on cybercrime.gov.in.
