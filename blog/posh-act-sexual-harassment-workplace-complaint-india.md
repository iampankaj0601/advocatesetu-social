# POSH Act: How to Complain About Sexual Harassment at Work

*Illustrative example:* Sana has been hearing uncomfortable comments from a senior colleague for weeks. A co-worker tells her to "just ignore it" because complaining will make things worse. What many people don't know is that Indian law gives a clear, time-bound way to complain.

That law is the **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013**, usually called the **POSH Act**.

## Who is protected?

The Act protects **any woman, of any age, whether employed or not**, who faces sexual harassment at a workplace. This includes domestic workers.

## Where do you complain?

| Situation | Where the complaint goes |
|---|---|
| Workplace has **10 or more employees** | The **Internal Committee (IC)** the employer must set up |
| No Internal Committee at the workplace | The **Local Committee** of the district where the incident happened |
| Prefer to file online | The Government's **SHe-Box** portal: shebox.wcd.gov.in |

On SHe-Box, a complaint is forwarded to the Internal or Local Committee registered on the portal, and you can track its status.

## Time limits to remember

- **Complaint:** within **3 months** of the incident. The Committee can extend this by **up to 3 more months**, recording its reasons in writing.
- **Inquiry:** must be completed within **90 days**.
- **Employer action:** the employer has to act on the Committee's recommendation within **60 days**.

## Relief during the inquiry

On a written request, the Committee can recommend interim relief, such as:

- transfer of the complainant or the respondent,
- leave of **up to 3 months** for the complainant, or
- stopping the respondent from supervising the complainant's work.

The Committee can also try conciliation if the complainant asks for it in writing, but **no monetary settlement** can be the basis of such conciliation.

## Employers have duties too

An employer who fails to set up an Internal Committee or otherwise comply with the Act can face a **penalty of up to ₹50,000**.

## Keep these records

- Dates, times and places of each incident
- Messages, emails or screenshots
- Names of anyone who saw or heard what happened
- A copy of your complaint and any acknowledgement or SHe-Box reference

## FAQs

**Does my office need to have an Internal Committee?**
Yes, if it employs 10 or more people. Otherwise the district's Local Committee handles complaints.

**Is there a deadline to complain?**
Generally 3 months from the incident, extendable by up to 3 more months by the Committee.

**Can I file the complaint online?**
Yes, on the SHe-Box portal (shebox.wcd.gov.in). In an emergency, call **112**.
