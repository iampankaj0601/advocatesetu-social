# Zero FIR in India: File an FIR at Any Police Station

"This is not our area, go to the other police station." Many people hear this when they try to report a crime. Under **Section 173 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023**, the place where the crime happened is not a reason to turn you away.

## What is a Zero FIR?

Information about a **cognizable offence** can be given at **any police station, irrespective of the area where the offence was committed**. This is called a Zero FIR. The police record it and then transfer it to the police station that has jurisdiction, which carries on the investigation.

This matters most when time is short: an accident on a highway, a theft while travelling, or a crime against a woman or child where every hour counts.

## Ways to report

| Method | What to know |
|---|---|
| In person at the police station | Information can be given orally; it is written down and read over to you |
| Electronically (e-FIR) | Taken on record once you **sign it within 3 days** |
| Emergency | Dial **112** first if someone is in danger |

## Your free copy of the FIR

You are entitled to a **copy of the FIR free of cost**. Ask for it and keep it safe; it carries the FIR number you will need to follow up.

## If the police refuse to register your FIR

Under **Section 173(4) BNSS**, you can send the substance of your complaint **in writing and by post to the Superintendent of Police (SP)** of the district. Keep a copy of the letter and the postal receipt. If that does not work, further remedies are available before the Magistrate; an advocate can guide you on them.

## Your rights if you are arrested

**Article 22 of the Constitution** protects every arrested person:

- You must be **told the grounds of arrest**
- You have the right to **consult a lawyer** of your choice
- You must be **produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours** (excluding travel time)

## Keep these records

- Your copy of the FIR (with FIR number and date)
- Copy of any written complaint and postal receipt for letters to the SP
- Photos, bills, messages or other evidence related to the incident
- Names of police officers you spoke to and the date and time

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I file an FIR where I live if the crime happened in another city?**
For a cognizable offence, yes. You can give information at any police station as a Zero FIR, and it will be transferred to the right station.

**Do I have to pay for a copy of my FIR?**
No. The informant gets a copy free of cost.

**What if I reported online?**
An e-FIR is taken on record once you sign it at the police station within 3 days.
