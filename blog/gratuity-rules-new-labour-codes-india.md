# Gratuity Rules in India Under the New Labour Codes

Gratuity is a lump sum your employer pays when you leave a job after years of service. Since **21 November 2025**, when the four Labour Codes came into force, gratuity is governed by the **Code on Social Security, 2020** (replacing the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972). Here is what changed and what stayed the same.

## Who is eligible?

- **Regular employees:** you need **five years of continuous service**. Gratuity is paid when employment ends, for example on retirement, resignation or superannuation.
- **Fixed-term employees:** you become eligible after **one year of continuous service**, on a proportionate basis. This is the biggest change under the new Code.
- **Death or disablement:** the minimum service period does **not** apply. On an employee's death, gratuity goes to the nominee, or to the legal heirs if there is no nominee.

## How much gratuity do you get?

The basic rule is **15 days' wages for every completed year of service**. The total is capped at **₹20 lakh**, the ceiling currently notified by the Central Government.

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Minimum service (regular) | 5 years |
| Minimum service (fixed-term) | 1 year, proportionate |
| Amount | 15 days' wages per completed year |
| Maximum | ₹20 lakh (as notified) |
| Payment deadline | Within 30 days of becoming payable |

## The 50% wages rule

"Wages" means basic pay, dearness allowance and retaining allowance. Under the Labour Codes, if your **allowances and other benefits exceed 50% of your total remuneration**, the excess is **added back to wages**. Since gratuity is calculated on wages, this can raise the amount for employees whose salary was mostly allowances.

## When must the employer pay?

Your employer must pay gratuity **within 30 days** of it becoming payable. If the payment is late, **simple interest** is payable for the delay. If there is a dispute about the amount, the matter can be taken to the competent authority under the Code.

## Keep these records

- Appointment letter or fixed-term contract, showing your start date
- Salary slips showing basic pay, DA and allowances
- Relieving letter or resignation acceptance with your last working day
- Copy of your gratuity nomination form
- Any written communication with your employer about gratuity

## Frequently asked questions

**I am on a one-year fixed-term contract. Do I get gratuity?**
Yes. Under the new Code, fixed-term employees are eligible after one year of continuous service, on a proportionate basis.

**Is gratuity paid if an employee dies before completing five years?**
Yes. The five-year condition does not apply on death or disablement; the amount goes to the nominee or legal heirs.

**What is the maximum gratuity?**
Currently ₹20 lakh, as notified by the Central Government.
