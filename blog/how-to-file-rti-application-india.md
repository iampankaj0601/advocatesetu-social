# How to File an RTI in India

Want to know what happened to your file, application or complaint in a government office? The **Right to Information Act, 2005** lets any citizen seek information from a public authority. Here is how the process works, in plain words.

## Where to send your RTI

Every public authority has a **Public Information Officer (PIO)**. Your application goes to the PIO of the office that holds the information.

- **Central government ministries, departments and public authorities:** you can file online at **rtionline.gov.in**. This portal is only for central bodies.
- **State government offices:** apply to that office's PIO, or through the state's own RTI portal where one exists.

## Fees

| Applicant | Application fee (central public authorities) |
|---|---|
| General applicant | **₹10** |
| Below Poverty Line (BPL) applicant | **No fee** (attach a copy of your BPL certificate) |

State governments set their own fee rules, so check the state's rules for state offices.

## How long the office has to reply

| Situation | Time limit |
|---|---|
| Normal request | **30 days** from receipt of the application |
| Information concerning a person's life or liberty | **48 hours** |

## If you get no reply, or are not satisfied

1. **First appeal:** file it within **30 days** with the First Appellate Authority, an officer senior to the PIO in the same office. No fee is charged for a first appeal. On rtionline.gov.in, an online first appeal can be filed against an RTI that was filed online.
2. **Second appeal:** if the first appeal does not resolve it, you can go to the **Information Commission** within **90 days**.

## Keep these records

- A copy of your RTI application and the fee receipt (or online registration number)
- Proof of the date the office received it
- The PIO's reply, or a note that no reply came within 30 days
- Copies of your first and second appeals

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I file an RTI online for a state office on rtionline.gov.in?**
No. That portal is only for central government public authorities. Use the state's process or portal instead.

**Do BPL applicants pay anything?**
Under the central RTI Rules, BPL applicants pay no application fee, but must attach a copy of their BPL certificate.

**How soon must the office reply?**
Within 30 days, or within 48 hours if the information concerns someone's life or liberty.
