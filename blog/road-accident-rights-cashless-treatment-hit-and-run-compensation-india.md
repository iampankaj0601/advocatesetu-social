# Road Accident Rights in India: Cashless Treatment, Hit-and-Run Compensation and More

Imagine Kavya, whose father is hit by a car that speeds away. A neighbour tells her, "The driver ran away. No compensation now. Just pay the hospital." (This is an illustrative example.) Many families believe this. But the law gives accident victims support even when the vehicle is never traced.

## 1. Cashless treatment up to ₹1.5 lakh

Under the **Cashless Treatment of Road Accident Victims Scheme, 2025**, a person injured in a road accident involving a motor vehicle can get **cashless treatment of up to ₹1.5 lakh per victim**, for **up to 7 days from the date of the accident**. The idea is simple: treatment should not wait for money.

In an emergency, first call **112** and get the injured person to the nearest hospital.

## 2. Compensation in hit-and-run cases

When the vehicle cannot be identified, the **Compensation to Victims of Hit and Run Motor Accidents Scheme, 2022** provides fixed compensation:

| Situation | Compensation |
|---|---|
| Death | ₹2,00,000 |
| Grievous hurt | ₹50,000 |

This is available even though there is no known driver or insurer. The application is made through the local claims enquiry authority (usually the sub-divisional or district administration) where the accident happened.

## 3. Helpers are protected: the Good Samaritan law

Many bystanders hesitate to help, fearing police trouble. **Section 134A of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988** protects a Good Samaritan who helps an accident victim in good faith from **civil or criminal liability**, and they **cannot be compelled to disclose their identity**. So helping someone reach a hospital is protected by law.

## 4. Where can a compensation claim be filed?

When the vehicle is known, a compensation claim is filed before the **Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT)**. Under **Section 166(2)**, you can file it where:

- the accident took place,
- the claimant lives or carries on business, or
- the defendant lives.

A 2019 amendment introduced a six-month time limit for claims, but in November 2025 the Supreme Court, as an interim measure, directed that claims should not be dismissed as time-barred while the challenge is pending. Since the position may change, it is wise to file early.

## Keep these records

- FIR or police report copy
- Hospital and treatment records, bills and discharge summary
- Medical/post-mortem report, where applicable
- Vehicle number, photos of the spot and names of witnesses (if available)
- Proof of income and age of the victim
- Identity and relationship documents of the claimants

## FAQs

**Can we get compensation if the driver fled?**
Yes. The hit-and-run scheme provides ₹2 lakh on death and ₹50,000 for grievous hurt.

**Will I get into trouble for taking a victim to hospital?**
No. Section 134A protects Good Samaritans who help in good faith, and they need not reveal their identity.

**Do I have to file the claim where the accident happened?**
No. You can also file where you live or carry on business, or where the defendant lives.
