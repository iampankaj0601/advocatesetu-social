# Rent Agreement in India: Registration, Stamp Duty and Receipts

Imagine Rohan, a young professional, moving into a new flat. The landlord hands him a three-year rent agreement typed on plain paper and says, "Just sign, no registration needed." (This is an illustrative example.) Before signing anything like this, it helps to know three basics.

## 1. When must a rent agreement be registered?

Under **Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908**, a lease of immovable property **for more than one year** (or from year to year, or reserving a yearly rent) must be **registered** with the Sub-Registrar.

This is the main reason so many residential agreements in India are written for **11 months**: a lease of one year or less does not fall under compulsory registration. A three-year lease, like the one in our example, does.

| Lease term | Registration |
|---|---|
| Up to 1 year (e.g. 11 months) | Not compulsory under s.17 (some states have their own rules) |
| More than 1 year | Compulsory |

## 2. Why stamp duty matters

Every rent agreement must carry the **stamp duty prescribed by your state**. Rates differ from state to state. Under the **Indian Stamp Act**, a document that is unstamped or under-stamped **cannot be admitted in evidence** until the missing duty and a penalty are paid. So an agreement on plain paper may cause trouble exactly when you need to rely on it.

## 3. Keep proof of every payment

Pay rent through a traceable channel (bank transfer or UPI) or collect a **signed receipt** every month. The same goes for the security deposit. Clear records help both tenant and landlord avoid disputes about what was paid and when.

## What about the Model Tenancy Act, 2021?

The Centre's **Model Tenancy Act, 2021** is a model law that states can choose to adopt. It suggests, among other things, a security deposit of up to **two months' rent** for residential premises and **24 hours' written notice** before a landlord enters the premises. These rules apply only where your state has adopted them, so check your state's law.

## Keep these records

- Signed copy of the rent agreement (registered copy, if the term is over one year)
- Stamp paper / e-stamp certificate
- Security deposit receipt
- Monthly rent receipts or bank/UPI statements
- Photos of the flat's condition at move-in and move-out

## FAQs

**Is an 11-month rent agreement valid?**
Yes. A lease of one year or less is not compulsorily registrable under Section 17, but it still needs proper stamp duty under your state's law.

**Can I just sign on plain paper?**
An unstamped agreement cannot be used as evidence until the duty and a penalty are paid, and a lease of more than one year also needs registration.

**Does the Model Tenancy Act apply in my state?**
Only if your state has adopted it. Check your state's rent or tenancy law.
